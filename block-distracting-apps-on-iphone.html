How to block distracting apps on iPhone and stay focused

You open your phone to check one thing and twenty minutes disappear. That is not a character flaw: feeds are designed to be hard to put down. The fix that works is to put a small, deliberate barrier between you and the apps that pull you away, for a set length of time, and then remove it automatically. Here is how to do that on iPhone, from the built-in options to a dedicated focus app.

Why “I’ll just be disciplined” stops working

  • Every check is a tiny decision. Saying no fifty times a day is tiring; by the afternoon you stop saying no.
  • The habit runs on autopilot. Many people unlock the phone and open the same app before they have even decided to.
  • Open-ended limits are easy to break. “Less social media today” has no start, no end and no moment where you notice you broke it.

A focus session solves all three: you decide once, the apps are blocked for a fixed time, and the session ends on its own.

When iPhone’s built-in tools are enough

iPhone already offers Screen Time with App Limits and Downtime, and Focus modes that silence notifications. They are a good start if you mainly want a daily cap on one or two apps, or fewer notifications during work hours.

Many people find a dedicated app easier when they want a clear start-and-stop session instead of a daily allowance, a different setup for studying, deep work and bedtime, or a visible countdown and a record of the focused time they have reclaimed.

Step by step with LockHour Pro

  1. Choose the apps that pull you away. Be honest: usually two or three apps cause most of the drift.
  2. Pick a focus mode from Quick Focus, Deep Work, Study Mode, Sleep Wind Down and Morning Reset, or set your own length with Custom.
  3. Start the session with one tap. The chosen apps are shielded immediately and unlock automatically when the time is up.
  4. Turn on Hard Mode if you tend to quit early. It adds a cooldown and a confirmation before you can stop, so ending a session is a choice, not a reflex.
  5. Keep the countdown in view. Widgets on the Home and Lock Screen show your progress, and a Live Activity counts the session down in the Dynamic Island.
  6. Review your week. Insights show today’s focused time, weekly charts, streaks and how many hours you have reclaimed.

Tips that make it stick

  • Start smaller than you think: two short sessions a day beat one ambitious session you skip.
  • Attach a session to something you already do, such as sitting at your desk or getting into bed.
  • Let the streak motivate you, but do not punish a missed day; start again the next morning.
  • Keep the phone out of reach as well. Blocking apps and adding distance work well together.
